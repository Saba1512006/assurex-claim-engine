Limitations

  • The claims are synthetic, generated from our own warranty policies. Real claims will need retraining and new calibration.
  • The image model reads the Claim Summary Card, which shows facts against policy limits. If an administrator changes a limit that the card draws, the image model must be retrained on new cards.
  • Neither model decides a claim on its own. When they disagree or are unsure, the claim goes to a person.
  • Rare situations, such as reporting a fault very late, have few training examples; the rule engine catches them instead.
  • Scores describe these test claims only. They are not a guarantee for any single claim.

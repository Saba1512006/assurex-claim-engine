{{ sec_head('limits', 9, 'Limitations') }}
  1. The claims are synthetic, generated from our own warranty policies. Real claims will need retraining and new calibration.
  2. The image model reads the Claim Summary Card, which shows facts against policy limits. If an administrator changes a limit that the card draws, the image model must be retrained on new cards.
  3. Neither model decides a claim on its own. When they disagree or are unsure, the claim goes to a person.
  4. Rare situations, such as reporting a fault very late, have few training examples; the rule engine catches them instead.
  5. Scores describe these test claims only. They are not a guarantee for any single claim.

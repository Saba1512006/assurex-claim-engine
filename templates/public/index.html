How a claim is decided

The same five steps run for every claim, in this order, and every step is stored with the result.

  1. Intake

    The customer or a service center files the claim; a checklist says what is missing before submission.

  2. Documents

    Receipts are read by OCR, files are checked by their contents and fingerprinted, and conflicts are flagged.

  3. Two models

    A Python classifier reads the claim record; a Teachable Machine model reads a picture of the same facts.

  4. Policy

    Sixteen checks from the category's warranty policy run: coverage, deadlines, exclusions, serials, repairs.

  5. Decision

    A decision table combines everything. When the models disagree or are unsure, a reviewer decides.
